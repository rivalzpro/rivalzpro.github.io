Crosshair Placement at Neck Level

Roblox player models stand at a uniform height across all Roblox Rivals maps. Pre-aiming your crosshair at the horizon line (neck/head height) cuts your flick distance by over 60%, allowing instantaneous headshots when opponents peek corners.

Inertia & Counter-Strafing

Unlike source engine games, Roblox avatar physics have slight momentum glide when releasing movement keys [A] or [D]. Tapping the opposite key brings your character to an immediate dead stop, eliminating weapon sway and maximizing first-shot pinpoint accuracy.

1:1 Focal Length Matching

Roblox Rivals scales your field of view down when zooming in with optics. Using our Sensitivity & Scope Scaler tab ensures your physical mouse travel distance matches your hipfire muscle memory across all optics without overshooting.

AR Vertical Pull-Down Rhythm

The Assault Rifle climbs vertically during continuous automatic fire. Pull down gently with your wrist in a steady downward stroke. If targets strafe laterally, combine smooth horizontal tracking while maintaining steady downward micro-drag.
